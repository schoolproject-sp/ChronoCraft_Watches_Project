Five Centuries of Portable Timekeeping

Before wristwatches became an everyday essential, portable timepieces began as spherical brass pendants worn around the neck in Renaissance Europe.

Historical Milestone: In 1904, Louis Cartier crafted one of the first purpose-built men's wristwatches so Brazilian aviator Alberto Santos-Dumont could check the time while flying without taking his hands off the controls.

Key Eras in Horological History

  1. 1510 — The Nuremberg Egg: German locksmith Peter Henlein invented early spring-driven portable clocks that fit inside a pocket.
  2. 1761 — Marine Chronometer H4: John Harrison built a sea-going timepiece accurate enough to calculate longitude on ocean voyages.
  3. 1926 — First Waterproof Case: Rolex introduced the Oyster case with a hermetically sealed screw-down crown.
  4. 1969 — The Quartz Revolution: Seiko unveiled the Astron, the world's first commercial quartz wristwatch, offering unmatched daily accuracy.
