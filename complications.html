What Is a Horological Complication?

In watchmaking, any function beyond displaying the basic hours, minutes, and seconds is known as a complication.

Gravity Defier: Invented by Abraham-Louis Breguet in 1801, a Tourbillon mounts the escapement and balance wheel inside a rotating cage to average out positional gravity errors.

Most Admired Mechanical Complications

  • GMT / Dual Time Zone: Adds an extra 24-hour hand so travelers can track home time and local time simultaneously.
  • Moonphase Indicator: Tracks the 29.5-day lunar cycle through an aperture on the dial, accurate for 122 years before needing adjustment.
  • Perpetual Calendar: Automatically adjusts for 30- and 31-day months as well as February leap years until the year 2100.
  • Minute Repeater: Uses tiny internal hammers and gongs to chime the exact hours, quarter-hours, and minutes on demand.
