Precision Engineering on the Wrist

From 16th-century pocket timepieces to modern automatic chronographs and atomic-accurate quartz movements, watchmaking blends micro-mechanical engineering with timeless craftsmanship.

Horology Fact: A traditional mechanical watch movement beats at 28,800 vibrations per hour (4 Hz), allowing the seconds hand to glide smoothly eight times every second.
