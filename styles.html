Classic Watch Categories & Tool Designs

Over the 20th century, specialized watches were engineered for deep-sea divers, pilots, racing drivers, and formal evening wear.

Safety Bezel Rule: A true dive watch features a unidirectional rotating bezel that turns only counter-clockwise, ensuring a diver never accidentally overestimates remaining oxygen time.

Four Signature Watch Styles

  • Dive Watches: Built with 200m to 300m water resistance, luminous dial markers, and sturdy stainless-steel bracelets for underwater legibility.
  • Racing Chronographs: Equipped with stopwatch pushers and a tachymeter bezel scale to measure average speed over a known distance.
  • Aviation & Pilot Watches: Oversized high-contrast dials with large crowns designed to be gripped while wearing flight gloves.
  • Minimalist Dress Watches: Ultra-thin cases paired with leather straps that slip effortlessly beneath a formal shirt cuff.
