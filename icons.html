Legendary Watchmaking Maisons

A look at four iconic watchmakers whose innovations shaped modern horology across Switzerland and Japan.

First Watch on the Moon: In July 1969, NASA astronauts wore the manual-wind Omega Speedmaster Professional during the Apollo 11 lunar landing.

Heritage Houses & Their Icons

  • Rolex (Geneva, Switzerland): Renowned for rugged chronometer precision and iconic tool watches like the Submariner and Daytona.
  • Omega (Biel/Bienne, Switzerland): Official timekeeper of the Olympic Games and creator of the legendary lunar Speedmaster and Seamaster lines.
  • Patek Philippe (Geneva, Switzerland): Celebrated for hand-finished grand complications, perpetual calendars, and heirloom pocket and wristwatches.
  • Seiko & Grand Seiko (Tokyo, Japan): Pioneers of the quartz revolution and the unique Spring Drive movement that combines mechanical power with electronic regulation.
